# Common Use-Cases in Customer Assistance

## E-commerce  
- Customers frequently inquire about product availability, return policies, shipping information, and order tracking.  

## Travel and Hospitality  
- Customers seek assistance with booking flights, hotel reservations, cancellation policies, and itinerary changes.

## Telecommunications  
- Common inquiries involve account management, billing issues, service outages, and plan upgrades.

## Financial Services  
- Clients often request help with account access, transaction issues, loan inquiries, and investment advice.

## Healthcare  
- Patients typically need assistance with appointment scheduling, insurance claims, and medication inquiries.

## Tech Support  
- Users frequently require help with troubleshooting hardware/software issues, account setup, and software updates.

## Education  
- Students and parents often ask about enrollment procedures, course offerings, and financial aid options.

## Key Customer Needs in Each Domain
- Information seeking
- Issue resolution
- Account management
